Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 18, 2026

Core Privacy Commitment: VoiceScribe is architected as a privacy-first, client-side application. We do not record, store, or sell your voice audio or dictation transcripts on any private servers.

1. Information We Collect and Process

VoiceScribe operates directly inside your web browser. Here is an exact breakdown of what happens to your data:

  • Speech Recognition Audio: Your microphone input is processed in real time through the standard browser Web Speech API (provided natively by Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, and Brave). The raw audio streams directly between your browser and the underlying operating system speech engine. VoiceScribe itself never intercepts or saves audio files to a remote database.
  • Local Transcripts & Notes: Any transcripts, notes, and auto-title settings you create are saved exclusively in your browser's LocalStorage on your device. Clearing your browser data deletes these notes permanently.
  • Usage Metrics: We do not track keystrokes, personal identification numbers, or identity profiles.

2. Third-Party Advertising and Cookies (Google AdSense)

To keep VoiceScribe 100% free for writers, students, and professionals worldwide, this website may display advertisements served by Google AdSense and third-party advertising partners:

  • Google and other third-party vendors use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other sites across the internet.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your browsing patterns and interests.
  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting the official Google Ads Settings.
  • Alternatively, you can opt out of third-party vendor cookies for personalized advertising by visiting AboutAds.info Choices or your regional equivalent.

3. Cookies and Web Storage

We use standard HTML5 Web Storage (localStorage) to remember your preferences (such as your chosen dictation language, font family, typography size, and dark/light theme). These cookies and storage keys are entirely local to your machine.

4. Children's Information (COPPA)

VoiceScribe does not knowingly collect any Personal Identifiable Information from children under the age of 13. If you believe that your child provided personal information through our website, please contact us immediately so we can promptly address it.

5. GDPR and CCPA Data Rights

Under international privacy regulations (including GDPR and CCPA/CPRA), users possess the right to:

  • Request disclosure of what data categories are gathered.
  • Request erasure of any personal records (all transcripts can be erased anytime via the "Clear All Notes" button in the app).
  • Opt out of any sale or sharing of personal data (VoiceScribe never sells user data).

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. Any amendments will be reflected on this page with an updated timestamp.
