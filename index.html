VoiceScribe: High-Performance Speech-to-Text Studio & Real-Time Voice Typing

VoiceScribe is a free, privacy-first browser speech-to-text dictation workspace. Dictate notes, articles, emails, and transcripts in 100+ languages with hands-free voice punctuation commands, client-side processing, and YouTube tab/audio transcription.

How to Use VoiceScribe (3-Step Quick Start)

  1. Select Your Language: Choose from 100+ languages including English, Hindi, Spanish, French, German, Japanese, and regional Indic dialects.
  2. Start Dictation: Click Start Dictation or press Ctrl+M to begin speaking naturally. Speak punctuation cues such as full stop, comma, new line, or question mark.
  3. Export & Save: Export your transcribed drafts as Markdown, plain text, PDF, or Microsoft Word documents with zero server storage.

Transcribe YouTube & Tab Audio Streams

VoiceScribe includes native system audio capture to transcribe YouTube videos, webinars, and meeting tabs directly into notes:

  • Live Tab Audio Capture: Share a Chrome tab with audio enabled to stream and transcribe speech using high-speed AI Whisper or Gemini Flash.
  • 100% Free Loopback (Stereo Mix): Route system sound to your browser input for unlimited, completely free live typing using Chrome’s built-in Web Speech API.
  • Audio & Video File Transcriber: Drop MP3, WAV, M4A, or MP4 recordings to generate complete text transcripts on demand.
